Introduction
------------
This mod adds a stone golem monster to the game.
The stone golem is an aggressive mob that will attack players on sight.

Features:
- Stone golem with 15-25 HP, 4 melee damage
- Spawns on stone and cobblestone
- Dogfight attack type (melee combat)
- Takes damage from water and lava
- Group attack behavior (nearby golems will help)

How to use:
1. Install the Mobs Redo (mobs) mod first.
2. Place this mod in the mods folder.
3. Enable both mods in your world configuration.
4. Stone golems will spawn naturally on stone surfaces.
